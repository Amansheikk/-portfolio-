My journey into software development began during 11th grade, when I wrote my first Python programs out of curiosity. What started as experimenting with simple scripts quickly turned into a genuine passion for building things that solve real problems.

Joining engineering opened an entirely new world. During my first year, I discovered web development and began learning how to transform ideas into complete digital experiences using modern frontend and backend technologies.
